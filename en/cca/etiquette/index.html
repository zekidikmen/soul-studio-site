Etiquette

Reservation Rules

Due to high demand at our Spa, we recommend booking your treatment or massage in advance.
Please be at the SPA reception at least 5 minutes before your scheduled start time. Please note that if you are late for your reservation, the duration of your treatment will be shortened accordingly. If you are late, we have to finish your treatment within the specified time so as not to disrupt the start time of the guest with the next reservation. Thank you in advance for your understanding.
If you wish to cancel your appointment for any reason, we kindly request that you notify the Spa reception at least 5 hours in advance for massage and treatment reservations, and at least 48 hours in advance for package program reservations. Failure to notify us will result in a 100% charge being added to your account.

What to Wear

You are kindly requested to wear swimwear, bikinis, or shorts. For your comfort, you can use the kimono and slippers available in your room. Towels are available in the SPA area.
We recommend using disposable underwear provided by us during your session.

Valuables

We recommend leaving your valuables in the safe in your hotel room. The hotel management is not responsible for any losses.

Choosing a Spa Treatment

All our spa services are listed on our menu. If you would like assistance and advice in making your selection, please consult the spa reception.

Noise & Sound

To ensure that both you and our other guests can enjoy a relaxing spa experience, we kindly request that you refrain from speaking loudly or making any disruptive behavior.

SPA Pools

Due to our hygiene regulations, you must shower before entering the pools in the SPA area. Also, jumping into the pools is dangerous and prohibited.

Age Limit

Our hotel concept allows us to accommodate guests aged 14 and over. Guests must be at least 18 years old to benefit from our facial treatments.

Mobile Phone Use

To ensure a quiet and peaceful environment in the spa areas, please leave your mobile phones in the safe located in your room.

Special Health Conditions

If you have a special health condition or are pregnant, please inform us during the booking process so that we can provide you with the most suitable and safest service. The sauna and steam bath temperatures in the spa range from 45˚C to 90˚C. We do not recommend the use of treatments with high heat and humidity levels for guests with heart, blood pressure, or respiratory problems, or pregnant guests. If used, the responsibility lies with the individual.

Personal Preferences & Requests

You can inform the spa reception staff and therapists of your preferences and special requests, such as room temperature or massage pressure level.

Products

You can bring the spa experience home by purchasing the natural oils and care products used during massages from the Callisto Shop located within the spa area.

Choosing a Therapist

If you prefer a private therapist, please inform the spa reception 48 hours in advance.

General

Before your massage, we recommend using the pools and steam baths. After using the SPA, we recommend showering to cleanse your body before starting your massage and treatment.
For SPA use, you can follow the steps in the "Recommended SPA Use Order and Durations" section in the SPA Menu.
You can prevent water loss from your body in high temperatures by drinking plenty of water. If you are not used to the heat, you may feel tired and sleepy at first. It will return to normal as your body gets used to it.
We recommend that you have eaten at least 1.5 hours before your massage.
We also recommend that you have a body scrub or a traditional Turkish bath scrub before some body treatments.
You should not use the wet areas of the SPA after some body and facial treatments. We kindly ask you to organize your daily SPA program taking this information into consideration.
We recommend that you relax in the relaxation areas in the SPA section after your massages and treatments. Guests who make noise or behave inappropriately in a way that disturbs other guests will have their SPA use and treatments terminated.
