Hawaiian Lomi Lomi

According to the ancient Hawaiian "Huna" philosophy, there are positive forces within every human being that can solve their problems. Lomi Lomi Massage is believed to help awaken these positive energies. It is a massage that kings and statesmen have before taking important decisions such as war. It activates the decision-making mechanisms with the relaxation it provides during the massage. The effects of the massage are felt within the next two to three days, not immediately after it is applied like other massages.
